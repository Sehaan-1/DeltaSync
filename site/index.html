Engine Subsystems

Engineered for Harsh Realities

⚡

FastCDC Chunking

Dynamic boundary normalization scans over 400 MB/s with zero heap allocations in the hot path. Deduplicates identical chunks across files and revisions.

🕰️

Vector Clock Causality

Complete Lamport partial-ordering over peer timelines. Deterministically distinguishes monotonic updates from concurrent branch divergences.

🛡️

Chaos-Tested Crash Safety

SQLite WAL mode with normal synchronous durability. Survived rigorous kill -9 stress cycles without a single byte of corruption.

🔒

TLS 1.3 Transport

Length-prefixed streaming sockets with mutual certificate pinning, bounded inbound queues (C=32), and BGP-4 collision tie-breaking.
